PRIVACY

About your visit.

This site uses Amplitude for analytics. Visits on jimkultgen.com and www.jimkultgen.com use the production project; local previews and other domains use a separate testing project.

Analytics and session replay

Amplitude collects page visits, link interactions, and browser and device information. It uses identifiers to connect activity across visits. Session Replay can capture interactions such as clicks, scrolling, and visible page content. Web experiments may show different versions of a page when configured.

Guides and surveys

Amplitude may show guides and surveys when configured. If you respond, Amplitude processes your answers and records the survey interactions.

Your preference

You can turn off Amplitude for this site in this browser. This preference is stored locally and takes effect after the page reloads. It does not delete data already collected.

Other services

This website uses your device’s fonts. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub take you to those services, which have their own privacy practices.
